Privacy · Last updated 18 November 2026

Your data. Kept simple.

This page explains what happens with your information when you visit seiranabdullah.me or send me a message. Short version: I only collect what I need to reply to you and, if you allow it, anonymous visit statistics. I never sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

Seiran Abdullah (legal name Seyran Gurbanov), freelance graphic & motion designer, Riga, Latvia.
Contact for any privacy question: seiranabdullah@gmail.com

2. Contact form

When you use the "Email me" form, I receive your name, email address and message. I use them only to reply to you and, if we work together, to discuss the project. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your message to my Gmail inbox (Google). Legal basis: steps before a possible contract and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

I keep messages only as long as needed for our conversation or project, and delete them on request.

3. Statistics (Google Analytics)

Only if you press ACCEPT in the cookie banner, the site loads Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd.) to count visits: pages viewed, time on site, approximate location (country/city), device type and which buttons were clicked (for example "WhatsApp" or "Download CV"). This helps me understand what visitors find useful. Google may process this data outside the EU under the EU standard contractual clauses. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can change your mind at any time with the button below.

Cookies set after consent: _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics, up to 2 years). Statistics are kept for up to 14 months.

4. Things stored in your browser

The site remembers two small things in your own browser, never on a server: your cookie choice (sa_consent) and whether the one-minute message was already shown (sa_min, deleted when you close the tab).

5. Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google servers. Links to WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Behance, LinkedIn, Instagram and Facebook open those services, and their own privacy policies apply there. Sounds on this site are generated in your browser and send no data anywhere.

6. Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct, delete or export your data, to limit how I use it, or to object to its use. You can withdraw consent at any time. Just write to seiranabdullah@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Latvian Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv).

7. Changes

If anything changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
